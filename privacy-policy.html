GARAGIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Overview

Garagio helps you track vehicle mileage, expenses, and maintenance. No account or login is required.

Data stored on your device

Vehicle details, mileage, expenses, and maintenance records you enter are stored locally on your device. Garagio does not provide cloud synchronization or storage of your complete records on our own servers. However, information submitted through app forms is also transmitted to our analytics provider as described below.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand app usage and improve Garagio. When you submit forms, all fields you enter may be sent to PostHog as analytics events. This can include vehicle details, mileage, expense amounts, maintenance information, garage names, notes, and other text you provide. We also collect screen views, app interactions, a randomly generated app installation identifier, and technical information associated with analytics requests (such as IP address and device information).

The installation identifier allows events from the same installation to be associated. It does not directly contain your name or email address, but analytics data is pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous. No account is required, but form fields may contain personal information you choose to enter. PostHog may process and store this information outside your country of residence.

In-app announcements

We use PostHog feature flags to show occasional announcements about Garagio updates, features, and requests for feedback. These announcements are not used to promote unrelated third-party products.

Data deletion

You can remove locally stored app records by clearing Garagio’s app data or uninstalling the app. Device backup settings may affect whether copies remain in your backups.

Deleting local records or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete analytics events already sent to PostHog. To request deletion of those events, contact us using Profile → Contact Developer in the app, or email dadi21cent@gmail.com. Include your installation identifier if available so we can locate the relevant events.

Data use and security

We use analytics to understand user needs, analyze vehicle and expense trends, and improve Garagio. We do not sell user data or use it for third-party advertising. Analytics is transmitted over encrypted connections and retained according to our PostHog settings and applicable requirements.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version will be available at this page. For questions or privacy requests, contact dadi21cent@gmail.com.
