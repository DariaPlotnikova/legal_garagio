GARAGIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Overview

Garagio helps you track vehicle mileage, expenses, and maintenance. No account or login is required.

Data stored on your device

Vehicle details, mileage, expenses, maintenance records, reminders, selected language and measurement settings, onboarding and announcement state, and cached fuel estimates are stored in private app storage on your device. Garagio does not provide cloud synchronization or store your complete records on our own servers.

Android backup is disabled for Garagio. You can export a plaintext JSON copy of the app database through Profile → My data → Export data. The export does not include analytics data. After export, the file is outside Garagio and is not protected by the app; you are responsible for storing it securely and choosing where it is saved or shared.

Analytics and diagnostics

We use PostHog Cloud in the European Union to understand how Garagio is used, fix problems, and improve the app. We send screen-open events, interaction events, event outcomes, and non-content form metadata. Form metadata may include whether a field was filled, a field name from a validation error, and selected options such as a reminder type, unit, or source. We do not send the text or numeric values you enter in forms, including vehicle names, garage names, notes, mileage, dates, fuel prices, currency values, titles, or expense amounts.

Garagio also sends a randomly generated app installation identifier, a session identifier, the event format version, and whether the app is a development or production build. PostHog creates a pseudonymous profile linked to the installation identifier. This identifier does not directly contain your name or email address, and Garagio has no account or login.

PostHog automatically receives technical information including app version, package name, and build; device manufacturer, model, name, and type; screen size and density; operating system and version; locale and timezone; network type and carrier; user-agent information; and whether the app is running in an emulator or was sideloaded. These attributes are also attached to the pseudonymous profile.

PostHog derives approximate location from the request IP address. This can include country, region, city, approximate latitude and longitude, accuracy radius, and timezone. Garagio does not request GPS or precise location permission. We do not use analytics data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell user data.

Garagio automatically sends diagnostics for uncaught Java/Kotlin errors and native crashes, and for some unexpected handled failures. These diagnostics can include an exception type, message, stack trace, issue metadata, and the app and device information described above. Garagio filters custom diagnostic properties and does not intentionally add form values, notes, filenames selected for import or export, or other user-entered content.

Automatic application-lifecycle capture, automatic screen-view capture, deep-link capture, push-notification capture, session replay, element-interaction capture, rage-click capture, and dead-click capture are disabled. Screen-open events are recorded by Garagio directly. Session replay is not recorded.

Feature flags and announcements

We use PostHog feature flags to configure parts of the app and to show occasional announcements about Garagio updates, features, and requests for feedback. Announcements are not used to promote unrelated third-party products.

Service providers and international processing

PostHog processes analytics, diagnostics, and feature-flag data on our behalf. PostHog and its service providers may process data in other countries where permitted by law and with appropriate safeguards. We do not share analytics data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

PostHog applies a one-year retention period to analytics and diagnostic events. Expired events are removed during periodic cleanup and may remain briefly after the retention window. Pseudonymous profiles and their properties are stored until you request deletion or we otherwise delete them; deleting a profile also removes its associated events. Aggregated or de-identified information may be retained longer. Session replay data is not collected.

Data deletion

You can remove all locally stored Garagio records by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. This does not automatically delete analytics events or the pseudonymous PostHog profile already associated with your installation identifier.

To request deletion of analytics data, use Profile → My data → Request data deletion before clearing the app data, or email dadi21cent@gmail.com. The in-app action opens a message prefilled with your installation identifier so we can locate the relevant events and profile. We will delete that data unless we are required to retain it by law. If you clear app data before sending the request, include the identifier in another way if you still have it.

Security

Analytics and feature-flag requests are sent over HTTPS. Remote links used by production configuration must also use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the data described in this policy.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be available at this page. For questions or privacy requests, contact dadi21cent@gmail.com.
